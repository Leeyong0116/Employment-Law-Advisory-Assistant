## 5. Industrial Court awards: design and A1 (parsing)

Design `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-industrial-court-awards-design.md`; plan `docs/plans/2026-11-13-a1-award-ingestion.md`.

**Author's decisions:** index the court's reasoning only (its statement of the law, findings and decision; not facts, evidence, submissions, addresses); a separate award lane after the statute gate (at most 2 award paragraphs; statutes never pushed out, gate on statutes only); cite "Award No. N of 2025, para P" with case names as published; privacy acceptable for a local FYP prototype (to mention in the report). The statute chunks are not re-embedded: awards are added beside them.

**Source survey (35 PDFs):** 9 in Bahasa Malaysia (1822, 1824, 1825, 1845, 1862, 1869, 1876, 1897, 1919; out, English-only corpus; 1862 and 1919 first looked English because the language sample hit an English quotation); 7 English awards without paragraph numbers (1815, 1823, 1826, 1828, 1829, 1877, 1909; out, "para N" not citable; 1909's numbers are a list of charges); **19 used**. `k.pdf` was Award 1923 of 2025 (renamed by the author). The file named 1912 holds **Award No. 1895 of 2025** (filename wrong at source; the registry declares the real number). Venues: KL 12, Perak 2, Johor, Penang, Kedah, Sabah, Sarawak 1 each. Sabah and Sarawak awards apply only the IRA.

**Build (`src/ingestion/awards.py`, TDD, 28 tests):** one chunk per numbered paragraph inside hand-checked `reasoning` ranges; a paragraph runs from marker k to the next number in sequence only, so quoted lists and other awards' paragraph numbers never split it; numbers the published award skips are declared (`skips`) after checking the PDF (1812, 1817, 1910, 1931), and an undeclared break is a validation problem. Output to `data/processed/award_chunks.jsonl`, separate from `chunks.jsonl`, so an index rebuild cannot mix awards into statute retrieval before the A3 lane exists.

**Problems found and fixed (each with a test from the real case):**
- Running headers ("19/4-491/22", "Page 1 of 58") and page numbers inside paragraphs: removed by position, as for statutes.
- Paragraph style "1." alone on a line (1812); award numbers written "1812 OF YEAR 2025" and "1900 / 2025".
- Statute names broken across PDF lines were missed, so 1923 and 1864 showed no statute applied.
- Headings glued to the end of the previous paragraph (49 chunks): moved out and kept as the next paragraphs' `section_title` (195 distinct titles, e.g. "THE LAW AND BURDEN OF PROOF"). The first rule removed text: a Malay cross-examination answer ("TAMIL Ya. Setuju") was taken as a heading and lost, the award version of a lost proviso. Final rule: decide on the whole trailing run of lines, take the longest part that is a heading, only after a finished sentence (never after a question), one case style, no full stop inside, a lone word only if it is a known heading.
- A line-end hyphen was first dropped ("berturutturut", "posttermination"): kept now; the awards are typeset from Word, which does not hyphenate words.

**Result:** 1,143 chunks (19 to 132 per award), validation clean. Every chunk's first and last 50 characters found in its PDF text (0 of 1,143 flagged). My read of a 6-chunk sample: complete paragraphs, quotations inside them, sensible section titles. Audit sample for the author: `data/eval/award_audit_sample.csv` (38 rows, 2 per award, seed 42), as the 60-chunk statute audit. Backend unit tests: 511 passed.

**Author's audit:** all 38 rows of `data/eval/award_audit_sample.csv` checked against the PDFs: **38/38 correct** on content, boundaries and page numbers (Y/N columns filled with Y, note "author check 2026-11-13"). Same standard as the 60/60 statute audit.

## 6. A2 + A3 plan (award index and award lane)

Plan `docs/plans/2026-11-13-a2-a3-award-index-and-lane.md`, draft for the author's approval; nothing built yet.

**Problem found while planning: one shared BM25 would move statute retrieval.** BM25 scores depend on corpus-wide statistics (how rare a word is, average chunk length). Adding 1,143 award paragraphs, full of words such as "dismissal" and "Claimant", changes those statistics for every statute query. Measured with the gold v1 questions (scratch script, BM25 only, no GPU): BM25 over statutes alone vs over statutes + awards with award hits removed, the statute top-20 list differs for **76 of 77** questions and the top 5 for 52. So "same BM25 as the statutes" and "gold v1 must not move" cannot both hold. Plan: same method (tokenizer, class, settings), two index files; the statute BM25 is rebuilt from the same statute chunks and stays identical. Dense search has no such effect: a cosine score depends only on the query and the chunk, so awards in the same table only need a filter (a new `doc_type` column, default `statute`, because awards share the IRA's jurisdiction "Malaysia").

**Other decisions proposed:**
- Statute vectors reused from the database rather than re-embedded. bge-m3 batches its input by length, so a different input list can change fp16 results in the last digits.
- The lane only in `hybrid_rerank` mode.
- Award chunks after the statutes and their expansion, outside the 12-chunk cap, `rank` null.
- The region rule read from the statute chunks' own jurisdictions; an unknown statute name is an error.
- The lane switched off in config until A4 brings prompt m4-v4.

Check for both milestones: gold v1 retrieval records equal per item, not only in the averages.

**Region rule on the real awards:** 2 of 19 awards apply the EA (1821, 1942; 180 chunks) and would be hidden for Sabah and Sarawak users; the other 17, including the Sabah (1900) and Sarawak (1891) awards, apply only the IRA.
